When I joined the team, the biggest challenge was that our on call rotation had no documented escalation path, so every incident turned into a scramble to find whoever happened to be awake. I spent the first two weeks just interviewing engineers and writing down what they already knew informally. Then I put together a runbook that mapped each alert to a specific owner and a specific set of first steps, and we ran two tabletop exercises to stress test it before relying on it for real. Within a month our mean time to acknowledge dropped by more than half, and just as importantly, people stopped dreading their on call weeks because they finally had something concrete to follow instead of guessing.
